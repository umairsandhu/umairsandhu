Markaz is Pakistan's largest social commerce & reselling platform — tens of thousands of resellers running a storefront out of a WhatsApp thread, with sourcing, payments and last-mile carrying the weight behind them.

---

### `~/now`

- **Rewiring Markaz to be AI-first** — not an AI feature bolted onto an ops team, but an operating model where the default way to answer a question is to *ask the system*, not open a spreadsheet.
- **AXIOS** — the internal system that makes the company queryable: a semantic layer over Postgres, so *"why did contribution margin drop in Multan last week"* is one question, not four analysts and a Tuesday. Markaz is customer zero; if it holds, it ships as a product.
- **Saudi Arabia** — a sourcing thesis, not a marketplace clone. SABER-compliant China→KSA rails for merchants who already have demand and can't get supply reliably. Five falsifiable tests, kill criteria written down before we started.

### `~/principles --borrowed`

> **Focus is a matter of deciding what things you're not going to do.** — *John Carmack*
> The GMV decline was a decision, not an accident. The list of things we stopped was longer than the list we started.

> **If you aren't sure which way to do something, do it both ways.** — *John Carmack*
